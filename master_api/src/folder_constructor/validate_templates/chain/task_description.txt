FROZEN STEPS:
- Steps marked with "frozen": true MUST NOT be modified during evolution.
- These steps are automatically restored from BASE_CHAIN_CONFIG after evolution.
- Do NOT change step_type, step_config, dependencies, or any fields of frozen steps.
- Only evolve steps that do NOT have "frozen": true.
- CRITICAL: Do NOT renumber frozen steps. Their step number is their identity.
  If you add new steps, use numbers that do NOT collide with frozen step numbers.
  Frozen steps will be force-restored to their ORIGINAL number, content, and dependencies.
